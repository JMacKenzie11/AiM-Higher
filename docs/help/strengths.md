# Strengths

The AiMS strengths assessment — a short set of questions that
produces a personal profile, plus team-level views for admins.

## Common things people do here

- **Take the assessment** — /strengths/welcome walks you through
  it once; results land at /strengths/results.
- **Explore team dynamics** (admins) — /strengths/teams groups
  people by their dominant strengths and highlights gaps.
