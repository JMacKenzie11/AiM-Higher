# Meeting transcript

Everything that was said in one meeting, as it was recorded, for everyone at your company.

## Reading it

Each line starts with who was speaking, as the recording named them. Where the recording only knew a speaker by a label, like "Speaker 2", the line shows that label; the Meeting Analysis tab of the meeting page has worked out who it was where it could.

The transcript is the recording's own words, so it can include mistakes the recording made in names and places. The meeting's write-up uses your company's own spellings.

*Meeting analysis*, at the top, takes you back to the write-up.

## Common questions

**Who can read this?** Everyone at your company, along with your AiMS guide. It's the same group that can read the meeting's write-up.

**Can I change it?** No. The transcript is the record of what was said. To correct the write-up or a commitment, use the meeting page or Commitments.
