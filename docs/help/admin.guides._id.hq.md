# Viewing another guide's Guide HQ

Read-only view of a specific guide&rsquo;s Guide HQ. Everything they
would see on their own /hq surfaces here — my commitments across
companies, the attention queue, the caseload, recent activity — but
every mutation control (resolve, reschedule, park, reassign) is
disabled.

## What you can and can&rsquo;t do here

- **Read** every panel exactly as the guide sees it.
- **Jump into** a company via its name link — that scopes you in
  the usual way; anything you change there is under your own
  identity.
- **Generate a Session Brief** for a company via the Prepare-for
  button. Briefs have no side effects; the brief author is
  recorded as you, not the guide being viewed.
- You **cannot** resolve, reschedule, park, or reassign any
  commitment from this page.

## Heads-up

- Works whether the target is an aims_guide or a system_admin
  carrying a coaching caseload.
- To take real action on a company, click into it and act there
  — the oversight view is intentionally passive.
- The banner at the top of the page names whose HQ you&rsquo;re
  looking at so it&rsquo;s never ambiguous.
