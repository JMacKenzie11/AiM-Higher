# Dashboard

Where your company opens. The page has two tabs under the welcome: **This week**, how the week is going, and **Dashboard**, how the quarter is going.

## This week

- **Recap video:** the latest recap of your weekly meeting, when there is one. When it finishes it goes back to the start, so press play to watch it again.
- **Follow-through last week:** of the commitments due last week, the share kept on time.
- **Done this week:** commitments due by this Friday that are already done, and who has done the most.
- **Week in review:** *What's worth knowing today*, a short written summary of the week so far.
- **Honor roll:** everyone who kept every commitment due last week.
- **Open commitments:** what's due by the end of next week, and anything past its date. Switch between *By day* and *By person*. Click a commitment to open it, the same as on Commitments.

## The Dashboard tab

Follow-Through Rate sits at the top, with three more numbers under it and the operating panels below.

- **Follow-Through Rate:** of the commitments closed this quarter, the share kept on time. A weekly repeating commitment counts once for each week it ran.
- **Strategic Progress:** average progress across your Focus Areas this quarter. Only commitments tied to a priority count here.
- **On Track:** how many of the open quarter's priorities are on track or complete, out of the total.
- **Open This Week:** commitments due by Friday that are still open.

Hover or tab to a number for a one-line definition.

### The panels

- **Critical Success Factors:** the last 13 weeks of every measure against its target, across every function. It shows once at least one value has been logged.
- **Wins this week, Streaks in flight, Where a conversation could help:** measures that hit target in the week just ended, measures at or above target for three weeks or more in a row, and measures that have dipped over the last three weeks. Each appears only when there's something to show.
::: feature ui_short_review_off
- **Focus Areas:** each one with its sponsor, status and progress. Click one to open it.
- **Follow-Through Rate Trend:** the last 12 weeks.
:::
- **Where to lend support:** everyone, sorted by Follow-Through Rate, lowest first. Click a name to open their scorecard.
::: feature ui_short_review_off
- **Recent wins:** the last commitments closed on time this quarter, with the priority each was linked to.
:::
::: feature ui_short_review
Focus Areas are on *Goals & Priorities*, and the week's wins open the weekly meeting's check-in.
:::

## What you can do here

- **See how the company is doing** this week and this quarter.
- **Open a Focus Area** or a person's scorecard from the panels.
- **Coach about anyone else at the company.** A *Coach* link sits beside each name but yours under *Where to lend support*.
- **Keep up with what you owe.** The bell at the top lists commitments that are due today or past due. If your company has Success Tracking on, on Fridays it also reminds you about numbers you haven't logged for functions you lead. Log them on **Critical Success Factors**, under Workspace.
:::

## Reading the Critical Success Factors board

There are two views. **Timeline** rolls each function's week into one square, so you can spot which functions are drifting. **Grid** shows a small trend line for each measure.

Each Timeline square is one function in one week, counting the measures that have a target:

- **All on target:** every one logged, and every one met.
- **At least one missed:** something came in under target. This outranks everything else, so a missed square can still hold numbers that were fine.
- **Some not logged:** nothing missed yet, but some numbers are still blank.
- **Nothing logged:** the week is empty.
- **No targets set:** nothing under this function has a target.

Hover a square to see the count behind it. The line at the top sums it up, for example *3 functions off target this week*. It says *Everything on target this week* only when every measure with a target was logged and met. If none of the measures on the board has a target, it says *No targets set yet*: add targets on **Critical Success Factors**. Click it to close or open the board, and it stays that way next time.

## Common questions

**Where do I log this week's numbers?** On **Critical Success Factors** in the sidebar. It saves one function at a time and tells you how many you have left.

**Why is a panel missing?** Panels only appear when there's something behind them. An empty quarter means empty panels.

::: role company_admin,aims_guide,system_admin
**Where's the setup checklist?** On **AiMS Implementation**.
:::

**The week in review says *No brief yet*.** It appears once there's enough activity this week. An admin's visit writes it each day, so on a day they haven't opened it you see the last one.

::: role company_admin,system_admin
**It stays empty for days.** Ask a system admin.
:::
