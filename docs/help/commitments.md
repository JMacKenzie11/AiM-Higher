# Commitments

This is where the work lives, one week at a time. Every commitment
is *owned*, *dated*, and either *open*, *kept*, or *missed*
(resolved after the due date, with a reason captured).

## Common things people do here

- **Mark a commitment kept** — click the green ✓ circle at the left
  of the row. A "Marked kept · Undo" chip appears in the row for a
  few seconds so a misclick reverses in one gesture. Overdue rows
  open a "what happened?" strip instead so the reason is captured.
- **Open a person's quick view** — click the owner name on any row
  to slide open a right-side drawer with their follow-through rate
  this quarter, kept/missed/open counts, a Coach button (admins and
  managers), and a link to the full scorecard. Reassigning to
  someone else happens inside the drawer too — one gesture deeper
  than before so an accidental click can't change ownership.
- **Reschedule** — click the due date to move the row and capture a
  short reason for the shift.
- **Add a commitment** — the always-live row at the bottom of
  *This week* creates one on save.
- **Edit the description** — click the commitment text to
  swap in an inline editor. Save (⌘/Ctrl-Enter) commits the
  change; Escape or Cancel reverts. Admins can edit any row;
  the assigned owner can edit their own on any status. The
  clarity dot re-scores automatically since the timeline and
  success verdicts depend on the wording.
- **Delete a commitment** — the trash icon sits to the right of
  the resolve check and is always visible when you have
  permission. Admins can delete any row; the assigned owner can
  delete their own open rows. A confirm dialog prevents accidental
  clicks.

## Heads-up

- **Needs attention** — anything past-week that's still open sits
  in its own red-titled section pinned above This week. The header
  pill counts the same set.
- The **clarity dot** to the right of the resolve circle shows
  whether the commitment has a stated timeline and a well-defined
  finish line. Click it to open the quick clarity editor. A
  commitment that passes both criteria doesn't need a refinement
  note — the field hides itself when both are YES.
- Action linking is frozen once a commitment resolves — this
  keeps action progress history from silently rewriting itself.
- Rows tagged *From meeting* were extracted from a transcript; the
  chip links to the analysis for admins.
