# Commitments

This is where the work lives, one week at a time. Every commitment
is *owned*, *dated*, and either *open*, *kept*, or *closed*
(missed with a reason).

## Common things people do here

- **Mark a commitment kept** — click the green ✓ circle at the
  start of the row. Overdue commitments open a "what happened?"
  strip instead so the reason is captured for the pattern.
- **Delete a commitment** — the red × next to the ✓ removes it.
  Admins can delete any commitment in their company; owners can
  delete their own open ones.
- **Reassign or reschedule** — click the owner name (or the due
  date) inline. Reassignment works across the roster and includes
  AiMS coaches for companies they're coaching.
- **Add a commitment** — the always-live row at the bottom of
  *This week* creates one on save.

## Heads-up

- Priority linking is frozen once a commitment resolves — this
  keeps priority progress history from silently rewriting itself.
- Rows tagged *From meeting* were extracted from a transcript; the
  chip links to the analysis for admins.
