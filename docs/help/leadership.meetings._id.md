# Meeting analysis

One team meeting: how it went, what was said and decided, and what the team committed to.

## How the page is laid out

At the top:

- **Meeting score**, out of 10, with a bar for each of the ten parts of the meeting. *See what earned each score* opens the breakdown. When the last meeting was scored the same way, a chip says how this one compares.
- **Try this next week**: one step, from the part of the meeting with the most room to grow.
- A row of quick numbers: decisions, new commitments, last week's commitments done, and issues worked.

Below are four tabs:

- **Summary** opens first. The meeting part by part, in the order of the AiMS weekly meeting: check-in, headlines, scorecard, goals and priorities, solving issues together, and commitments. Each part shows its score. Each issue shows which of the 4Ws the meeting reached (What, Want, Way, Who), and where one wasn't reached, the question to ask next time. Beside it: the decisions, who spoke and how much, and where the company's values showed up.
- **How it was run**: the score's breakdown (see below).
- **Commitments**: the commitments and issues that came out of the meeting, and last week's commitments with where each one stands.
- **Transcript**: everything that was said.

Each tab has its own link. Add `#summary`, `#how-it-was-run`, `#commitments` or `#transcript` to the page address to send someone straight to it.

A commitment where nobody named a day shows **By next meeting** instead of a date.

::: role team_member,company_admin,aims_guide,system_admin
## A coaching call

A coaching call's page has *Coaching call analysis* above its name and a line saying who can see it: the company's coaches, its admins and anyone they've added. *Meeting Analysis* holds the analysis. *Issues and commitments* lists what the call drew out. Nothing goes on the company's lists by itself: as the company's admin or coach, click *Add* beside a commitment, or *Add to open issues* or *Resolved in meeting* beside an issue, and it's added like one from a team meeting. *Coaching notes* is hidden on a call for now. *← Coaching calls* takes you back to the tab.
:::

::: role team_member
## What you can do here

- **Read the meeting.** Everything on the page is open to everyone at your company: the summary, the score and how it was made, the commitments and the transcript.
- **See the commitments it created.** Each one is also on Commitments, where its owner closes it out or reschedules it.
- **Pick up one thing for next week.** *Try this next week* is a single step the whole team can take.
:::

::: role company_admin,aims_guide,system_admin
## What you can do here

- **Send each commitment where it belongs.** On the Commitments tab, when Automated Commitment Tracking is off, the *Commitments identified* card lists what the meeting agreed to. For each one, link it to a Priority, link it to a Functional Area, or choose *Convert to issue*. A chip then shows what happened: *Added to* the priority or function, *Captured as commitment*, or *Issue created*. It stays that way when you come back.
- **Deal with the issues it raised.** *Issues identified* lists the open questions from the meeting. For each one:
    - *Resolved in meeting* closes it straight away, for something the team already talked through. It lands on the resolved list on the Issues page marked *Resolved in meeting*.
    - *Add to open issues* puts it on the open list on the Issues page, where someone can add the desired outcome, a commitment and an owner.
  These are on the Commitments tab. Whichever you click first wins, and clicking twice won't add it twice. A tick in a circle means it was closed in the room; a plus in a circle means it went on the list.
- **Reword anything tagged *Needs rewording*.** It mentions something personal about someone, and AiMS couldn't reword it for you. Add it as usual. The commitment or issue it becomes carries the tag until someone edits its text on Commitments or Issues.

## Possible duplicates

A small *Possibly already captured* badge means a commitment or issue looks a lot like one already open from the last 14 days. Click it to see the match and a link to where it lives, so you can check before adding. It's a hint: add the row anyway if the likeness is a coincidence. Items this meeting already created don't count.
:::

## How the score is made

The score follows the AiMS meeting facilitation rubric. It has ten parts, each scored 1 to 5:

1. **Positive check-in**: a real check-in where everyone shares, and wins are celebrated by name.
2. **Headlines**: a short headline from every area, in the meeting or sent ahead, with bigger conversations moved to Issues.
3. **Critical Success Factors and data**: numbers updated before the meeting, the whole scorecard reviewed, and off-track measures flagged or moved to Issues.
4. **Goals and priorities**: each goal named with where it stands, last week's priority commitments reviewed, and new ones with an owner and a date.
5. **Operational commitments**: the same, for commitments not tied to a priority.
6. **Generative dialogue**: open, curious questions, and issues chosen, reframed and worked through to a commitment.
7. **Focus and decisions**: staying on the agenda, and closing each topic with a decision, a small group to decide, or a spot on next week's agenda.
8. **Meeting container**: ideas heard and understood before they're challenged, everyone invited in, and strengths and values named.
9. **Structure**: every part of the meeting covered, closing by confirming what was agreed or pointing the team to AiMS HQ.
10. **Engagement**: everyone taking part, with no one voice holding the floor.

The ten scores add up to a total out of 50, and that divided by 5 is the score out of 10.

Each part is made of checks, and a part earns a level only when every check at that level and below it is met. Open a part on *How it was run* to see **What went well** and **To do it even better**. Each check says where it came from:

- **AiMS HQ**: your team's own records, such as commitments, measures and headlines sent ahead.
- **Counted**: counted from the meeting, such as who spoke and which items came up.
- **Judged**: read from the conversation. A judged check that went well shows the words it rests on.

A commitment due **By next meeting** counts as having a date.

Meetings analysed before 27 November 2026 keep the score they were given at the time, without the ten-part breakdown.

::: role system_admin
## Running a meeting again

*Reanalyze meeting* sits at the bottom of the page. It only appears on a meeting that has no commitments or issues created from it, such as one that failed or came back empty. It replaces the write-up and finds the commitments and issues again.

While it runs, an *Analyzing this meeting* banner shows above the tabs and the button is hidden. Refresh in a minute or two to see the result. If it comes back empty, running it again usually works.
:::

::: role portfolio_admin
## What you can do here

- **Read the meeting**: the summary, the score and how it was made, and the transcript.
- **See the commitments and issues** it produced on the Commitments tab.
- **Send commitments and issues where they belong**, in a company where a system admin has made you its admin. You get the same *Commitments identified* and *Issues identified* controls as the company's admins. *Commitments identified* shows only when Automated Commitment Tracking is off.
:::

## Common questions

**A commitment from this meeting looks wrong.** Open it on Commitments to reassign it, reschedule it or fix the wording. If it's tied to an issue, change it on the Issues page instead.

**A commitment came out Unassigned.** Aimee only gives a commitment to someone she can tell was at the meeting. If she couldn't tell who said it, or it named someone who wasn't there ("I'll see if John can do it"), it comes out Unassigned so it doesn't land on the wrong person's list. Open it on Commitments and pick the owner.

**The summary says "an unidentified speaker".** The recording labels people by number, and Aimee only names someone when she's confident who it was.

::: role team_member,company_admin,aims_guide,system_admin
**Who sees this page?** Everyone at your company can read the summary, the commitments, the score and how it was made. Commitments from the meeting show on each owner's Commitments list and scorecard, like any other commitment.
:::

::: role company_admin,aims_guide,system_admin
**A meeting has commitments but needs a better summary.** Ask your AiMS contact. It can be rewritten without changing the commitments or issues.

**Why is there no score?** Meeting Facilitation Review may be off for your company, the recording may have been too short or patchy to score, or the meeting hasn't been analysed yet. Your AiMS contact can have it redone without touching the commitments or issues.
:::
