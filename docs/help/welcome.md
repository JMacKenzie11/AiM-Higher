# Welcome

Your company's week on one page: the recap video from the latest leadership meeting, how last week's commitments went, and what is coming up.

## The recap video

A short video recap of the week, made from your latest leadership meeting. Press play to watch it. When a new recap is ready it takes the old one's place, and the page says *New recap this week*. Until the first one is ready, the space says so.

## Follow-through last week

Of the commitments due last week, the share kept on time, with how many that is.

## Past due

How many open commitments are past their due date, and who holds the most.

## Honor roll

Everyone who kept every commitment due last week. **Owned it** means they also marked each one done themselves; **Clean sweep** means every one was kept. When nobody kept every one, the people closest to it are shown instead.

## Open commitments

Everything open and due by the end of next week, plus anything past its date. Show them **By day** or **By person**. A long list shows its first six, with **Show more** for the rest. Your own have a coloured edge on the left. Press a commitment to open it, the same as on Commitments, to mark it kept, change its date or add a note.
