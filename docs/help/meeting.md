# Weekly meeting

::: feature ui_meeting_mode
The weekly leadership meeting in one view, for the screen everyone is looking at. It walks the agenda in order, and the timer at the top right shows how long you've been going.

## The steps

- **Check-in.** First, mark who's here: click the circle beside each person's name and it fills with a tick. Click it again to take the mark off. It saves as you go, and the count underneath shows how many are here. Then start with what went well. The question at the top is a simple one to go round the room, and it changes from week to week. Under it are the commitments kept in the last seven days and, when there are any, the critical success factors that met or beat their target for the week just ended.
- **Headlines.** Each functional area gives its update: what happened last week, what worked, what's coming, and what the area needs support on. Keep commitments and numbers for their own steps. Every area has a block showing last week's headline beside this week's. Type this week's in the box; it saves when you click away.
- **Numbers.** Only the critical success factors worth talking about: off target, or not logged for the week just ended. The rest are counted underneath.
- **Strategy.** The quarter's priorities that aren't finished, the ones behind first, with who owns each and how much is still open on it. Click one to open it.
- **Commitments.** Everything open this week and past due, grouped by person, so each person can go in turn. Close one out with its circle, or add new ones at the top of the card.
- **Solve.** Open with the question at the top: what do you need support on, and what haven't you been able to solve on your own? *Add an issue* puts each one on the list. Drag the list into the order you want to talk them through; the Issues page keeps that order. Click one to open it in the panel, talk it through, and add the next commitment there.
- **Close.** A reminder that commitments live here and get marked complete as you go, then how many people were here and what changed while the meeting ran: commitments kept and added, issues resolved and raised.

Press *Next* at the foot of the card to move on, or *Back* to go back. You can also click any step in the row at the top to jump to it. Whatever you change here is the same as changing it on its own page.

## Common questions

**Does this record the meeting?** No. It shows your company's work and lets you act on it. To get a summary, upload the meeting's transcript; it shows on *Meeting Summaries*.

**Is attendance kept from week to week?** Yes, one meeting a week. If you open the meeting again later that week, the ticks you made are still there. Next week starts with nobody marked.

**Can I change last week's headline?** No. Last week's is there to read against this week's. Only this week's box is open.

**Who can open it?** Everyone in the company. What each person can change is the same as everywhere else, and anyone in the company can write a headline.
