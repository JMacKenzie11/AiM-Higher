# Meeting transcripts

This page takes you to Companies, where meeting transcript settings live.

## Where to go instead

- **Connect Google Drive for a company.** Open the company from Companies and go to the *Connections* tab. Connect Google on the *External connections* card.
- **Add folders or aliases.** Once Google is connected, use the *Meeting transcripts* card on the same tab.

::: role system_admin
- **See meetings that haven't been matched to a company.** Use *Unrouted meetings* at the bottom of Companies. It only shows when a meeting is waiting, and you pick a company on each row.
:::
