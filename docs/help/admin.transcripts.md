# Meeting transcripts

This route redirects to `/admin/companies`. The transcript surface
has moved into each company's own settings page — the platform-wide
Google account and unrouted queue live on `/admin/companies`, and
each company's sources, meetings, and aliases live on
`/admin/companies/[id]`.

## Where to go instead

- **Connect Google Drive, add folders, add aliases** — open a
  company from `/admin/companies`, scroll to the *Meeting
  transcripts* card on its settings page.
- **See platform-wide unrouted meetings** — the transcripts
  panel at the bottom of `/admin/companies`.

## Why this URL still exists

Old bookmarks. The redirect keeps them working while training
everyone on the new location.
