# Meeting transcripts

This page takes you to Companies, where meeting transcript settings live.

## Where to go instead

- **Connect Google Drive, add folders or add aliases for a company.** Open the company from Companies and scroll to the *Meeting transcripts* card on its settings page.
- **See meetings that haven't been matched to a company.** Use the transcripts panel at the bottom of Companies.
