# Issues/Solutions

Your company's list of open issues. Name the issue, decide what you want, and commit to the next step that moves it forward.

## Reading the list

Each issue sits on its own line: what the issue is, and *What we want*. Its commitments are listed underneath, finished ones first, then the ones still open, each with who owns it and when it's due. An empty line at the bottom is ready for the next commitment.

A commitment made here also shows on Commitments, tagged *From issue*, and counts toward the owner's follow-through and the scorecard like any other.

The counts at the top (*Open*, *No commitment yet*, *Resolved this quarter*) always cover the whole list, so they don't change when you filter.

::: role team_member
## What you can do here

- **Add an issue.** Type it into the add row and press *Add*.
- **Work on issues you added.** Click the title to rename it, click *What we want* to set the outcome, drag the handle on the left to move it up or down the list, and add commitments to it.
- **Resolve an issue you added.** Tick the round check at the left of its line.
- **Look after your own commitments.** They work exactly as they do on Commitments: the circle opens *Mark kept*, *Reschedule* and *Park*, you can click the text to edit it or the owner's name to hand it on, and *Undo* stays on screen for 30 seconds. Rescheduling asks you for a short reason.
- **Delete your own open commitment** with the trash can beside it.

To change an issue someone else added, ask them or your company admin.
:::

::: role company_admin,aims_guide,system_admin
## What you can do here

- **Add, rename and resolve any issue**, and set *What we want* on any of them.
- **Reorder the list.** Drag the handle on the left. Everyone at the company sees the same order. Team members can move only the issues they added.
- **Add a commitment for anyone.** Pick the owner when you add it.
- **Close out, reschedule, reassign or mark missed** any commitment on an issue, without giving a reason.
- **Delete an open issue.** Use the trash can beside its round check. It can't be undone. The commitments on it stay on their owners' lists; they just lose the link to the issue. A resolved issue can't be deleted.
- **Turn meeting issues into real ones.** See *Issues from a meeting* below.
:::

## What you can do here

Read every issue and its commitments, and filter the list. Changes are made by the company's own people.

## How to add a commitment to an issue

1. Type the next step into the empty line under the issue.
2. Change the due date if you need to. The owner is you unless an admin picks someone else.
3. Press *Add*, or press Cmd/Ctrl+Enter. Clicking away from the line also saves it.

Once it's there, click any part of it to change it. It saves when you click away.

## Resolving an issue

Tick the round check at the left of the issue's line and confirm. The issue moves off the open list, and the commitments still open on it are marked kept, which counts toward follow-through. The confirm box tells you how many will close. Parked and repeating weekly commitments are left alone.

So only resolve when the work has actually landed. Nothing is resolved for you: when the last commitment is done, decide whether the issue is solved. If it isn't, add another commitment and carry on.

If a commitment was closed by mistake, reopen it from Commitments. The issue stays resolved.

## Resolved issues

Resolved issues are listed at the bottom, one line each. Click the arrow beside one to see every commitment it took to get there.

Some have no commitments. *Resolved in meeting* means the team talked it through in a meeting and no follow-up was needed. Others say that no commitment was ever raised.

## Filtering

- **Assigned to** shows issues where that person has an open commitment. Issues with no commitment drop out; the *No commitment yet* count covers those.
- **Status** shows open issues, resolved ones, or both.
- **Source** shows issues that came from a meeting, or ones added here by hand.

::: role company_admin,aims_guide,system_admin
## Issues from a meeting

A meeting summary lists the unresolved questions the team raised under *Issues identified*. None of them come here on their own. For each one, pick:

- **Add to open issues.** It joins the open list, ready for an outcome, a commitment and an owner. The row then shows *Added as issue*.
- **Resolved in meeting.** It goes straight to *Resolved issues*. Use it when the team settled it in the room. The row then shows *Resolved in meeting*.

Whichever you click first sticks. A *Possibly already captured* badge means something similar is already on Issues/Solutions or Commitments. Click it to see the match before you add a second one. It's only a hint, so add it anyway if it's different.

An issue tagged *Needs rewording* mentions something personal about someone, and AiMS couldn't reword it for you. Add it as usual; it keeps the tag here until you edit the title so it says only what needs solving.
:::

## Common questions

**What's the coloured dot next to a commitment?** It shows whether the commitment is clear: a due date someone agreed to, and a clear finish line. It works the same as on Commitments. Click it to see why.

**Can I reopen a resolved issue?** No. If the problem comes back, add a new issue.

**What happens to the commitments when an issue is deleted?** They stay on their owners' lists. Only the link to the issue goes.
