# Your profile

Edit your name, position, and your Strengths + Superpowers. Role
and company assignment aren't editable here — those live with
admins.

## Common things people do here

- **Update your strengths** — type into the trailing empty row and
  a new empty row appears automatically. Save at the bottom.
- **Change your position** — free-text field. Feeds the roster
  display and the AI coach's context about who you are.
