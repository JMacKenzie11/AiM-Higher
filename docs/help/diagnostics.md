# Capacity Diagnostic

Run a three-week Capacity Diagnostic for a client: the people in scope, their tasks and answers, the conversations, the Capacity Map and the first win, all in one place.

The pages are on no menu. Go to `/diagnostics` for every engagement, or `/diagnostics/clients` for the businesses you run them for. **Diagnostics** and **Clients** at the top of each switch between the two.

## Add a client

On **Clients**, select **Add a client** and fill in the business, the owner, its size and where it is. A client here is not a company on AiMS HQ. **Edit** on the client's page changes the details. **Archive** puts a client away without deleting anything, and **Restore** brings it back.

## Start a diagnostic

On the client's page, select **Start a diagnostic**. It opens on the first step. Each step is one card with **Back** and **Next** at the foot. The steps along the top go to any step directly.

1. **Pre-flight.** List the tools the group uses and the IT contact, then tick what is done. **Next** waits for **Access secured**: no access by kickoff means the kickoff moves.
2. **Kickoff.** Enter the kickoff and readout dates, the guarantee, the scope and the Capacity Destination. Add the people in scope as a list, one per line: name, role, email. Then edit the owner's pledge, select **Copy** to paste it into their email, and **Mark as sent** once they have sent it.
3. **Survey.** Select **Enter survey** beside a person and type in their answers: who they are, up to ten tasks with hours, energy and judgment, and the three open questions. After **Save survey** the drawer moves on to the next person without one. Under it, **Every task** lists everyone's tasks, biggest first. Enter the volume check (units a week, minutes per unit) against the biggest ones, set data risk and build hours, and select **Roll up titles** so the same work done by different people shares one name. You can change any name it gives.
4. **Conversations.** The guide is on the left; read the opening line to each person first. Pick the person and type your notes on the right; each box saves when you leave it. Tick **Had a conversation**. To keep a line for the readout, select the words in the notes and press **Pull a quote**. Under **Lines for the readout**, **Approve** the ones the owner may see and choose whether a name goes with each. A line has no name unless you give it one.
5. **Build.** The Capacity Map shows every task by energy and judgment, sized by hours. A filled dot is volume-checked; a hollow one is the person's estimate. The shaded corner is the candidates, scored in the table below it, with the top next wins under that. Select **Choose first win** to pick a pattern and the task it serves and enter the baseline. Once it is in use, enter the minutes per unit after and the Friday that ends its first full week: the measured hours back, the recovery rate, the projection and the guarantee fill in. Record the midpoint check with the owner at the bottom.

**Archive** at the foot of a diagnostic puts it away.

## Who sees what

Only system admins on AiMS HQ can open these pages. Individual ratings, answers and notes stay with you. The owner sees combined results only.
