# Function

Everything one function owns, on its own page: its name, where it sits on the chart, who's in the seat, and its responsibilities. When Role Descriptions is on for your company, it also has Decision Rights, Competency Indicators and a *Role Description* card at the bottom.

*Back to chart* at the top returns you to the Functional Chart. *Part of* under the name opens the function this one sits under, and *Sub-functions* lists the ones under it.

::: role team_member,portfolio_admin
## What you can do here

- **Read the function.** Who's in the seat, what it sits under, and every responsibility it carries.
- **Move around the chart.** Open the function above it from *Part of*, or one below it from *Sub-functions*.
- **Read its role description**, once one has been made. Click *View role description* on the *Role Description* card.

Changes are made by your company admin.
:::

::: role company_admin,aims_guide,system_admin
## What you can do here

- **Change the details.** *Details* holds the name, *Sits under* and *In the seat*. Change what you need and click *Save changes*. The button stays grey until there's something to save.
- **Move it to the top.** Choose *Top level (no parent)* under *Sits under*. A function can't sit under itself or one of its own sub-functions, so those aren't offered.
- **Add and edit responsibilities.** Type into *Add a responsibility* and press Enter. Click one to edit it, or use the bin to delete it. These save straight away.
- **Add decision rights and competency indicators**, with Role Descriptions on.
- **Delete the function** with *Delete function* at the bottom. It goes for good, with its sub-functions and everything under them. A confirmation spells out what goes with it first.

## How to publish a role description

1. On the *Role Description* card at the bottom, check the readiness list: Title, Responsibilities, Critical Success Factors with targets, Decision Rights and Competency Indicators. Fill any gaps.
2. Click *Create role description* the first time. After that it reads *View role description*.
3. On the role description page, click *Publish version*, add a note if you like, and click *Publish*.
:::

## Common questions

**Is this the same as the panel on the chart?** Yes. Clicking a box on the chart opens the same details in a panel over the chart. This page shows them on their own.

**Where are the critical success factors?** On the *Critical Success Factors* page. That's where you add them and log this week's value.
