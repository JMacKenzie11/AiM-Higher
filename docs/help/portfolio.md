# Portfolio

Your home as a portfolio admin: every company on this instance, one
card each, with the three numbers that say how each one is doing.
It is an oversight view. You can read everything inside a company
and change nothing that a company produces.

## What's on a card

- **Scorecard** — the company's overall AiMS Implementation score
  out of 10, with the number of disciplines it covers underneath.
  **Read that second line before comparing two cards.** An overall
  is an average across whichever disciplines have a score, so a
  company measured across four is not ranked against one measured
  across eight. The card tells you which is which rather than
  leaving you to assume.
- **Priorities** — how many of this quarter's priorities are on
  track or complete, as a percentage and as a count. A dash means
  no quarter is open, or the open one has no priorities yet.
  Zero means there are priorities and none of them are on track;
  those are different answers and the card shows them differently.
- **This week** — the share of this week's commitments kept on
  time. The week ends Friday **in that company's own timezone**,
  not yours, so this matches what the company sees on its own
  dashboard.

Click a company's name to scope into it.

## What you can do here

- **Open a company** — clicking the name scopes you in and lands
  you on its dashboard. Every entry is recorded: who, which
  company, and when.
- **Create a company** — the form at the bottom of the page. A new
  company arrives with its chart roots and an open quarter already
  set up, so somebody can start using it immediately.

## What you can do once you're inside a company

You can read everything: the dashboard, the plan, commitments,
measures, issues, meetings, the chart, the foundation. You cannot
write any of it, and the buttons that would do so are not shown
rather than shown and refused.

What you *can* change is the company itself, from its settings
page:

- its name, timezone and industry
- which features it has turned on
- archiving it

You cannot delete a company, and you cannot create another
portfolio admin or a system admin. Inviting people works for
company roles only — team members and company admins.

## Common questions

**Why can't I edit a priority / resolve a commitment / change a
measure?** By design. This role sees across the whole portfolio
and does not do the work inside it. The company's own admins and
guides own that.

**Why does one company show a dash where another shows a number?**
A dash means there is nothing to report yet — no quarter open, no
commitments due this week, no scorecard computed. It is not zero.
Zero means the thing was measured and came out at zero.

**Why don't I see Guide HQ, or the platform tools?** Those belong
to coaches and to system admins. Your equivalent is this page.

**Changing a company's timezone moved its numbers. Is that a
bug?** No. Every weekly and daily figure is bucketed by date in
the company's clock, so moving the clock re-dates history: counts
either side of the old midnight read differently afterwards. The
change is recorded with your name against it.

**I archived a company and it disappeared from here.** Archived
companies drop off this page. Reactivate it from its settings
page, which stays reachable while you are scoped into it.
