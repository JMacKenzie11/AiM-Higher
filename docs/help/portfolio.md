# Portfolio

Every company on this instance, one card each, with the three numbers that say how each one is doing.

::: role portfolio_admin
## What you can do here

- **See how every company is doing** at a glance.
- **Open a company.** Click its name and you land on its dashboard. Each time you open a company, it's recorded: who, which company and when.
- **Join a company's team** where you work. Tick companies on the *Company access* card and press **Update**.
- **Create a company** with the form at the bottom of the page. It arrives with its chart and an open quarter already set up, so people can start straight away.
:::

::: role system_admin
## What you can do here

This is the quickest read of how every company is doing. Your home is still Guide HQ; you reach this page from **Portfolio** in the sidebar.

- **Open a company** by clicking its name.
- **Set each portfolio admin's company access** on the *Company access* card. Tick the companies and press **Update** on their row.
- **Make a portfolio admin a company's admin.** Once a company is ticked and saved for them, tick **Company admin** beside it. It saves straight away. Untick it to take the admin rights away; they stay on the team.
- **Create a company** with the form at the bottom of the page. It arrives with its chart and an open quarter already set up.
:::

## What's on a card

- **Scorecard:** the company's overall AiMS Implementation score out of 10, from its latest weekly score. Under it you see the date the score was taken (for example *Score as of Friday Sep 26*) and how many disciplines it covers. It updates once a week, so it can be a few days old. A new company shows *No score yet* until its first weekly score. Check the disciplines line before comparing two cards. A company scored across four disciplines isn't directly comparable with one scored across eight.
- **Priorities:** how many of this quarter's priorities are on track or complete, with the quarter named. A dash means no quarter is open, or it has no priorities yet. 0% means there are priorities and none are on track.
- **This week:** the share of this week's commitments kept on time. The week ends Friday in that company's own timezone, so it matches what the company sees on its dashboard. *Nothing due this week* is different from 0%.

## Company access

As a portfolio admin you can always read everything in every company on this instance. Ticking a company puts you on its team: you can own commitments and priorities there and close them out, and you show up on its Team list as *team member*.

A system admin can also make you a company's admin. Then, in that company, you can do everything its company admin can, and you show up on its Team list as *company admin*. The card shows **Company admin** beside those companies. Only a system admin can turn it on or off, and who did it and when is recorded.

Unticking a company takes you off its team. If you own open commitments there, they go to **Unassigned** so someone else can pick them up. The card shows how many beside each company and asks you to confirm first. Commitments you've already closed keep your name.

Every change is recorded with who made it and when. A company admin in one of your companies can also remove you from their Team list. That ends your access there and nothing else.

::: role portfolio_admin
## Inside a company

You can read everything: the dashboard, the plan, commitments, measures, issues, meetings, the chart and the foundation. Unless a system admin has made you that company's admin, the buttons that would change things aren't shown, apart from on your own commitments and priorities.

From the company's settings page you can:

- change its timezone, industry and features
- archive it

You can add people to a company as team members or company admins, from its Team page. You can't delete a company, or create a portfolio admin or system admin.
:::

## Common questions

**Why does one company show a dash where another shows a number?** A dash means there's nothing to report yet: no open quarter, nothing due this week, or no score yet. Zero means it was measured and came out at zero.

**I archived a company and it disappeared.** Archived companies drop off this page. Reactivate it from its settings page.

::: role portfolio_admin
**Why can't I edit a priority, close a commitment or change a measure?** You need company admin access in that company. Otherwise the work inside it belongs to the company's own admins and guides.

**Why don't I see Guide HQ or the platform tools?** Those are for AiMS Guides and system admins. This page is your equivalent.
:::

**I changed a company's timezone and its numbers moved.** Weekly and daily figures are counted by date in the company's own clock. Moving the clock moves where each day starts and ends, so some counts shift. The change is recorded with your name.
