# Meeting Summaries

Your company's leadership meetings, newest first. Open one to read what was discussed, what was decided, and the commitments that came out of it.

::: role team_member
## What you can do here

- **Read a meeting.** Click *View* on any meeting marked *complete*. You'll see the summary and the commitments it created.
- **See what's on its way.** A meeting marked *pending* or *analyzing* is still being written up. That usually takes a few minutes.
- **Upload a transcript, if you're your company's AiMS champion.** Use *Upload a transcript* at the top of the page. It works the same way as it does for admins.

## Common questions

**Why can't I open some meetings?** You can open a meeting once it's marked *complete*. If one says *failed*, let your company admin know.

**Why is the list empty?** No meetings have come through for your company yet. Your company admin or AiMS champion can upload one, or connect where your recordings are saved.
:::

::: role company_admin,aims_guide,system_admin
## What you can do here

- **Read a meeting.** Click *View* on any meeting marked *complete* to see the summary and the commitments it created.
- **See how each meeting was run.** When Meeting Facilitation Review is turned on, each finished meeting shows a facilitation score out of 10. It's there to help you run better meetings, so it's never shown in red.
- **Upload a transcript.** Use *Upload a transcript* at the top of the page. Click *Choose file* and pick a .docx, .txt or .vtt transcript. Set *Held on* to the day of the meeting, then click *Analyse meeting*. It shows in the list straight away and is ready in a few minutes. Your company's AiMS champion can do this too.
- **Run a failed meeting again.** The reason a meeting failed shows beside *failed* in the Status column. Click *Try again* on that row. The meeting goes back to *pending* and is usually ready in a few minutes.

## Common questions

**Who can see these?** Everyone at your company: the summaries, the facilitation scores, and each meeting's transcript (*Read the transcript* on the meeting page).

**Can I upload a meeting we already have?** No. If the same transcript is already in the list, you'll be told so and it isn't added twice.

**Why is nothing showing up?** You can upload a transcript yourself. Meetings also come in from the Google Drive folders and Teams meetings connected on your company settings page. For a Drive folder, check it is connected and the recording was saved into it. For a Teams meeting, check it is connected and was run with transcription on.

**A meeting has said *pending* for a while.** It usually takes a few minutes. If it hasn't moved, use *Check now* on your company settings page.

**Why is there no *Try again* on a failed meeting?** It says *failed (dismissed)*. Someone set that meeting aside on purpose, so it isn't run again.
:::
