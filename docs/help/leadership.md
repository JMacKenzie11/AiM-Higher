# Meeting Summaries

Your company's leadership meetings, newest first. Open one to read what was discussed, what was decided, and the commitments that came out of it.

::: role team_member
## What you can do here

- **Read a meeting.** Click *View* on any meeting marked *complete*. You'll see the summary and the commitments it created.
- **See what's on its way.** A meeting marked *pending* or *analyzing* is still being written up. That usually takes a few minutes.
- **Read your coaching calls, if you've been given access.** A *Coaching calls* tab, with a lock, sits beside *Team meetings* once your coach or a company admin adds you. It holds your coach's calls with your company's leaders. Click *View* on a call to read it, or use *Add a coaching call* to upload one: choose the transcript file, set *Held on* and click *Add call*.

## Common questions

**Why can't I open some meetings?** You can open a meeting once it's marked *complete*. If one says *failed*, let your company admin know.

**Why is the list empty?** No meetings have come through for your company yet. Your company admin can upload one, or connect where your recordings are saved.

**Who can see the coaching calls?** Your coach, your company's admins, and anyone they've added. The line at the top of the *Coaching calls* tab names everyone. Aimee never reads them, and nothing from a call goes on your company's lists.

**How do I get access to the coaching calls?** Ask your coach or a company admin to add you. You'll get a notification when they do.
:::

::: role company_admin,aims_guide,system_admin
## What you can do here

- **Read a meeting.** Click *View* on any meeting marked *complete* to see the summary and the commitments it created.
- **See how each meeting was run.** When Meeting Facilitation Review is turned on, each finished meeting shows a facilitation score out of 10. It's there to help you run better meetings, so it's never shown in red.
- **Upload a transcript.** Use *Upload a transcript* at the top of the page. Click *Choose file* and pick a .docx, .txt or .vtt transcript. Set *Held on* to the day of the meeting, then click *Analyse meeting*. It shows in the list straight away and is ready in a few minutes.
- **Run a failed meeting again.** The reason a meeting failed shows beside *failed* in the Status column. Click *Try again* on that row. The meeting goes back to *pending* and is usually ready in a few minutes.

## Common questions

**Who can see these?** Everyone at your company: the summaries, the facilitation scores, and each meeting's transcript (*Read the transcript* on the meeting page).

**Can I upload a meeting we already have?** No. If the same transcript is already in the list, you'll be told so and it isn't added twice.

**Why is nothing showing up?** You can upload a transcript yourself. Meetings also come in from the Google Drive folders and Teams meetings connected on your company settings page. For a Drive folder, check it is connected and the recording was saved into it. For a Teams meeting, check it is connected and was run with transcription on.

**A meeting has said *pending* for a while.** It usually takes a few minutes. If it hasn't moved, use *Check now* on your company settings page.

**Why is there no *Try again* on a failed meeting?** It says *failed (dismissed)*. Someone set that meeting aside on purpose, so it isn't run again.
:::

::: role company_admin,aims_guide,system_admin
## Coaching calls

A *Coaching calls* tab, with a lock, sits beside *Team meetings*. It holds your coach's calls with the company's leaders. The company's coaches and admins always see them, along with anyone they add. The line at the top of the tab names everyone who can.

- **Choose who else can see them.** Click *Manage access* beside that line. Coaches and admins are listed under *Always*. To add someone, choose them under *Add a person* and click *Add*; they get a notification with a link to the tab. Click *Remove* beside someone to take their access away. Either takes effect straight away.
- **Add a call.** Under *Add a coaching call*, choose the transcript (.docx, .txt or .vtt), set *Held on* to the day of the call, and click *Add call*. It's ready in a few minutes. Anyone who can see the calls can add one.
- **Read a call.** Click *View*. The page is laid out like a meeting's: the summary, what the call drew out, and coaching notes.
- **Run a failed call again** with *Try again*, as for a meeting.

A coaching call stays on this tab. It never appears under *Team meetings*, Aimee never reads it, and nothing from it goes on the company's lists by itself. Its page lists the commitments and issues it drew out, and the company's admins and coaches can add each one with the button beside it.

**Who can't see them?** Everyone else at the company, unless they're added. A system admin who doesn't coach the company can't see them either.
:::
