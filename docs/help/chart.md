# Accountability chart

The chart is the org: who owns what. Every function is a box with a
leader, outcomes it's responsible for, and success measures that
tell you whether it's healthy.

## Common things people do here

- **Add a function** — click *Add function* under a parent to grow
  the tree. New companies start with Visionary and Integrator seed
  boxes at the top.
- **Assign a leader** — click the leader pill on a function to pick
  from the roster. Leaders can be reassigned any time.
- **Track outcomes + measures** — open a function to see its
  outcomes and edit weekly success measures.

## Heads-up

- **Delete function** on the function detail page removes the
  function and cascades to its sub-functions, outcomes, measures,
  and recorded weekly values — it's a hard delete. A branded
  confirmation dialog spells out the cascade before the click
  lands so a stray tap can't wipe a tree.
