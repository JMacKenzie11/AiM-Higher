# Ask Aimee

A thinking partner for whatever you're working through: a decision, a conversation to prepare for, someone who isn't on AiMS, or your own leadership. Your conversations are private to you unless you invite someone in.

## What you can do here

- **Start a conversation.** Click *New conversation*, type what's on your mind and send.
- **Ask how to use AiMS.** In a plain conversation, ask where something is or how to do it: "where do I add a quarterly priority?" Aimee answers from the help for your role and links you to the page. She only links to pages you can open.
- **Use a guided agent.** Inside a conversation, pick an agent from the agent picker beside the title. Each one opens with a few starter questions: click one, or type your own. You can change the agent until you send your first message. After that it stays for the whole conversation.
- **Pick up where you left off.** Every conversation stays in *Recent conversations*. Ones you started about a person show their name, and open on that person's coaching page.
- **Share a conversation.** See *Sharing a conversation* below.
- **Archive a conversation.** Click *Archive* on its row to tuck it out of the way.
- **Find a lesson.** When your company has Classroom, ask Aimee to suggest one: "what should I watch on facilitating a weekly meeting?"

Conversations other people have shared with you are listed under *Shared with you*.

## Agents

The agent picker shows the agents open to you. These are open to everyone:

- **Prepare a hard conversation.** Walks you through getting ready to talk to a specific person. Ends with a script you can copy or refine.
- **Navigate an emotionally charged conversation.** Uses the LEAD Model for a moment when someone is already upset. Ends with a script, and you can role-play it if you like.
- **Ask great questions.** Helps you write generative questions for a conversation, meeting or one-to-one.

::: role team_member
If you head up a function on the Functional Chart, you'll also see **Role Description Creator**. If you're your company's AiMS champion, you'll also see **Debrief a meeting**.
:::

::: role company_admin,aims_guide,system_admin
You'll also see:

- **Functional Chart Builder.** Walks you through building your company's Functional Chart: the core functions, their top responsibilities and the two seats above them. It ends with a proposal and an *Apply to Chart* button.
- **Role Description Creator.** Writes a role description for a seat on the chart, or for a role that isn't on it.
- **Debrief a meeting.** Talks through what your last leadership meeting showed about how the team works.

## How *Apply to Chart* works

Apply only adds. It never changes or removes anything already on your chart:

- Functions you already have stay as they are. New ones are created.
- Responsibilities you don't have yet are added to the matching function. Ones you have are left alone.
- The two top seats keep their current names. If the proposal suggested different ones, the summary tells you so you can rename yours.

The summary tells you exactly what changed. You can revise the proposal and press Apply again. Nothing is added twice.

If a proposal shows *Couldn't read that chart proposal*, press *Fix the proposal* and Aimee writes it out again.

You can share a chart conversation with a team member for their input. They can reply, but only people who can edit the chart can apply it.
:::

## Sharing a conversation

In a conversation you started, click *Share this chat* at the top. Search for someone and choose:

- **Collaborate.** They can reply to Aimee in the same conversation.
- **Read-only.** They can follow along.

Either way they see every message, from before and after they joined. You can share only with people at your company and the AiMS Guides assigned to it.

Only you can invite people, change their access, remove them, rename the conversation or archive it. Anyone you've shared with can open the same button and click *Leave this chat*. Once a conversation is shared, each message shows who sent it.

::: panel
## Aimee's panel, on every page

Aimee's icon sits in the bottom-right corner of every page. Click it, or press **Ctrl + .** (Ctrl and the full stop, on a Mac too), to open her panel beside the page you're on. On a computer you can keep working in the page. On a phone the panel fills the screen until you close it.

- **For you.** When someone shares a conversation with you, it's listed here with their name, and a number shows on Aimee's icon. *Open the chat* opens it in the panel. A conversation about a person opens on their coaching page instead.
- **A conversation.** Aimee greets you and suggests a few questions about the page you're on. Click one, or ask anything. The panel picks up your last panel conversation, and *New conversation* starts another. It stays with you as you move between pages.
- **She knows the page you're on.** On a page about one thing (a person, a function, a goal, a priority, a meeting) she can read its name and description, so you can ask "what is this for?" On the Functional Chart that includes the function open beside it. She only sees what you could open yourself.
- **The panel doesn't add to what Aimee remembers about you,** though she still uses what she already knows. For coaching you want her to remember, start a conversation on the Aimee page instead.

If you're your company's AiMS champion, *For you* also shows Aimee's invitations to talk a meeting through. Click *Talk it through* to start.

Panel conversations are listed here with your others.
:::

## Common questions

**Who can see my conversations?** Only you, unless you share one. Admins, your manager and AiMS Guides can't see them.

**Where do I see what Aimee remembers?** In the Memory card on your profile page. You can correct or delete any of it.

**Why does Aimee take a moment before answering?** She looks things up first: past commitments, strengths results, earlier conversations. While she does, the reply area tells you what she's reading. A first message usually takes longest.

**Aimee didn't know something obvious about my company.** She only knows what she can see in AiMS, and she never makes numbers up. Check the page the information should be on.

::: role aims_guide,system_admin
**A conversation is missing from my list.** The list shows the company you're currently in. Check which company you're in before assuming it's gone.

**I opened a conversation from another company and my company didn't change.** That's expected. The conversation opens in its own company, and you stay where you were. To move, pick the company from Guide HQ or the Companies list.
:::

::: role company_admin,aims_guide,system_admin
**Why can't someone at my company see the Functional Chart Builder?** It's for company admins, system admins and AiMS Guides assigned to the company. If someone who should have it can't see it, ask AiMS to check their role and, for a guide, which companies they're assigned to.
:::
