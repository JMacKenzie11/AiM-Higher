# Coach conversations

Private coaching threads about a specific person. Only *you* see
your own threads — no other admin, no manager, and never the
subject themselves.

## Common things people do here

- **Start a thread about someone** — the *About* mode drafts
  coaching notes on a person you manage or coach.
- **Continue a self thread** — the *Self* mode is for your own
  reflection about your role.

## Heads-up

- Threads are permanent by design. Even the subject can't read them
  — the whole point is a private space for coaches.
