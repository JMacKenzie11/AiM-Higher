# Role Descriptions

Every role description your company has written, newest first.

A role description says what one seat owns, what it is held to week
by week, what it can decide, and what excellence looks like in it.
You write one with Aimee, in conversation, and save it here.

## What's on the page

Each row is a **role**, not a version. A role you've rewritten four
times is one row, showing the newest version's number, the day it was
saved and who saved it.

**Roles on the Functional Chart** link to that function's role
description page, where the older versions live and where you can
publish or export it.

**Roles that aren't on the chart** stay as a plain line, because
there's no function page to send you to. That's the reason this page
exists: without it, a role description for a role you deliberately
kept off the chart would have nowhere to be found.

## How to write one

::: role company_admin,aims_guide,system_admin
1. Go to *Ask Aimee* and pick **Write a role description** from the
   agent list, under **People**.
2. Two ways in: a seat that's on your Functional Chart, or a role
   that isn't. Pick the one you mean; Aimee asks different questions
   for each.
3. Answer one question at a time. Aimee already knows your Functional
   Chart and your One-Page Plan, so she starts from what's there and
   asks you for the rest.
4. When every section is agreed, she hands you the finished document
   as a card.
5. **Save** puts it here and adds a version. **Download** gives you a
   Word file. **Copy** gives you plain text. Download and Copy work
   whether or not you've saved.
:::

::: role team_member
As a team member you can read the role descriptions your company has
saved. Writing them is an admin or guide job.
:::

## Common questions

**Does saving overwrite the last one?** No. Every save adds a
version, and nothing is ever written over. If you ask Aimee to change
something and she hands you a new card, saving that card is version
two.

**Can I write one for a role that doesn't exist yet?** Yes, and it's
worth doing. Describing the seat before you hire for it is the point.
Pick the off-chart option and tell Aimee which functions the role
supports, or that it supports none.

**Why isn't my role description here?** Nothing is saved until you
press Save on the card. Aimee proposes; you decide.

**Where did Decision Rights and Competency Indicators go?** Decision
rights are a section of the role description now. Competency
indicators became *What excellence looks like*, which ties each of
your core values to what it looks like when someone is doing this job
well.
