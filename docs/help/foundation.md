# One-Page Plan

The company's identity and direction on a single page: why you exist,
where you're going, who you serve, and how you'll know it's working.
Sections stack top to bottom: **Purpose → Vision → Core Values →
Differentiators → Ideal Customer → Focus Areas → Success Metrics**.

## What you can do here

::: role team_member
As a team member, you can:

- **Read the whole plan** — every section renders in the same order
  the company uses to introduce itself.
- **Jump between sections** — the chip nav under the hero stays
  pinned as you scroll; click any chip to glide to that section.
:::

::: role company_admin,aims_guide,system_admin
As an admin or guide, you can also:

- **Edit Purpose and Vision** — each has an inline Edit toggle that
  swaps the card into edit mode without leaving the page.
- **Add or remove items** in Core Values, Strengths &
  Differentiators, Ideal Customer, and Key Success Metrics — every
  numbered card carries Edit and Delete; each section has an
  *+ Add* affordance at the bottom.
- **Edit any list item** inline via the card footer's Edit action.
:::

## How to edit a section

1. Click the *Edit* button in the card footer (Purpose, Vision) or
   on the numbered card (list items).
2. Make the change and Save. The read view returns in place.
3. For list sections, use the *+ Add* disclosure at the bottom of
   the section to add a new item.

## How to keep Focus Areas up to date

Strategic Focus Areas render here as a **read-only preview**. The
write side lives on the Plan page — click through to
`/plan` to add, edit, or archive Focus Areas and they'll show back
up here.

## Common questions

**Why can't I edit Focus Areas on this page?** They're managed on
the Plan page so the cascade (Focus Areas → Annual Goals →
Priorities) stays in one place. The preview here is intentional.

**How careful should I be with these edits?** Foundation content is
company-wide and rarely changes. Every edit updates the whole
roster's mental model — worth a moment of thought before saving.
