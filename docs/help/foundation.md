# One-Page Plan

Your company's identity and direction on one page: why you exist, where you're going, what you stand for, who you serve and what you're focused on. The sections run in order: Purpose, Vision, Core Values, Strengths & Differentiators, Ideal Customer Profile and Focus Areas.

## Getting around

The links under the page heading jump straight to each section.

::: role team_member
## What you can do here

- **Read the whole plan**, in the order your company uses to introduce itself.
:::

::: role company_admin,aims_guide,system_admin
## What you can do here

- **Edit Purpose and Vision.** Click *Edit* on the card, make the change and save. The card switches back to reading view in place.
- **Add, edit or delete Core Values and Strengths & Differentiators.** Each numbered card has *Edit* and *Delete*, and each section has an add button at the bottom.
- **Add or remove Ideal Customer Profile entries**, under best-fit and psychographic. Each entry has a delete button, and each list has its own add button.

Changes here are seen by everyone at the company, so it's worth a moment's thought before you save.
:::

::: role portfolio_admin
## What you can do here

Read the whole plan. Changes are made by the company's own people.
:::

## Focus Areas

Focus Areas show here as a preview. They're managed on Goals & Priorities, alongside the goals and priorities that sit under them. Anything added or changed there shows up here.

## Common questions

**Why can't I edit Focus Areas on this page?** They live on Goals & Priorities, so Focus Areas, Goals and Priorities stay together in one place.
