# Classroom

A shared library of lessons and video trainings authored by AiMS.
Browse by category, open a lesson to see its trainings, and click
any training to watch the video and read the notes.

## What you can do here

- **Browse a category** — every category card groups lessons by
  theme (Foundational, Facilitation, Growth, and so on).
- **Open a lesson** — the lesson card lists every training inside.
- **Watch a training** — YouTube and Vimeo videos embed inline.
- **Download supporting files** — some trainings include PDFs,
  decks, or worksheets under "About this training".
- **Ask Aimee for a recommendation** — Aimee can search the
  library from Ask Aimee. Try "what should I watch on facilitating
  a weekly meeting?".

## How to find something to watch

1. From the landing page, scan the categories for a theme that
   matches what you're wrestling with.
2. Open a lesson to see the trainings under it.
3. Click a training to watch the video and read the notes.

## Common questions

**Why don't I see the Classroom link in the nav?** The Classroom
feature has to be enabled for your company. If it's off,
`/classroom` redirects to the dashboard. A system admin can turn
it on from company settings.

**Where are drafts?** Drafts are hidden — only lessons and
trainings marked *published* by AiMS show up here.

**Is the library company-specific?** No. The library is shared
across every company with Classroom enabled — the same lesson is
visible to everyone.
