# Classroom

A shared library of lessons and video trainings authored by AiMS.
Browse by category on the landing page, open a lesson to see the
trainings inside it, and click any training to watch the video and
read the notes.

## Common things people do here

- **Browse a category** — click any lesson card. Categories group
  lessons by theme (Foundational, Facilitation, Growth, etc.).
- **Watch a training** — inside a lesson, click a training to open
  the viewer. YouTube and Vimeo videos embed inline.
- **Download supporting files** — some trainings include PDFs, decks,
  or worksheets. They live below the "About this training" section.
- **Ask Aimee for a recommendation** — Aimee can search the library
  when you're talking about something the content covers. Try
  "what should I watch on facilitating a weekly meeting?"

## Heads-up

- Only lessons and trainings marked "published" by AiMS show up here.
  Drafts are hidden until publication.
- The library is shared across every company that has the Classroom
  feature enabled — the same lesson is visible to everyone.
