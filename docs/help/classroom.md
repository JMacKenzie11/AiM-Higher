# Classroom

A shared library of lessons authored by AiMS. Each lesson is
broken into **sections**; the section rail lives on the left of
the lesson page so you can jump between them without losing your
place. Videos and rich text live directly in the section body —
click any video thumbnail to play in place.

## What you can do here

- **Browse a category** — every category card groups lessons by
  theme (Foundational, Facilitation, Growth, and so on).
- **Open a lesson** — landing on a lesson opens its first section.
  Use the left rail to walk through the rest.
- **Watch a video** — thumbnails are clickable; the player only
  loads when you click, so a section with several videos doesn't
  spin up multiple players at once.
- **Deep-link to a specific section** — the URL updates as you
  move between tabs, so a copied link jumps a colleague straight
  to the right section instead of the top of the lesson.
- **Download supporting files** — some sections include PDFs,
  decks, or worksheets attached below the body.
- **Ask Aimee for a recommendation** — Aimee can search the
  library from Ask Aimee. Try "what should I watch on facilitating
  a weekly meeting?".

## How to find something to watch

1. From the landing page, scan the categories for a theme that
   matches what you're wrestling with.
2. Open a lesson — you'll land on its first section.
3. Read/watch the section, then use the left rail to advance.

## Common questions

**Why don't I see the Classroom link in the nav?** The Classroom
feature has to be enabled for your company. If it's off,
`/classroom` redirects to the dashboard. A system admin can turn
it on from company settings.

**Where are drafts?** Drafts are hidden — only lessons and
sections marked *published* by AiMS show up here.

**Is the library company-specific?** No. The library is shared
across every company with Classroom enabled — the same lesson is
visible to everyone.

**A video won't play.** Check whether the section's video is
YouTube or Vimeo — some corporate networks block one and not the
other. If the thumbnail loads but the player doesn't, that's
usually the culprit.
