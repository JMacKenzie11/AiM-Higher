# Coach HQ

Your home base as an AiMS Coach: your own commitments, the companies that need you this week, and a brief to prepare each coaching session. It covers only the companies you're assigned to coach.

## What you can do here

- **Close out your own commitments.** *My commitments* lists every commitment you own, across all your companies, with the company named on each row. Close out, reschedule and park work the same as on Commitments.
- **See who needs you.** *Needs your attention* is your call list for the week, most urgent first. Each row names the reason: a scorecard drop, a low or falling Follow-Through Rate, a weak facilitation review, a priority more than 14 days past due, or a meeting recording that looks like it belongs to that company but hasn't been matched to it.
- **Check your companies.** *Your companies* has one row each: scorecard, Follow-Through Rate, open quarter and the date of the last finished meeting.
- **See what's happened lately.** *Recent activity* shows the latest meetings summarised, reviews finished, and quarters opened or closed.
- **Prepare for a session.** Click *Prepare for {company}* on a row in *Your companies*.
- **Bring in your coaching calls.** *Your coaching calls folder* reads the Google Drive folder your calls with every client are saved in, and files each call under the right company. *Calls to sort* holds the ones it couldn't place.

## How to prepare for a coaching session

1. Find the company in *Your companies*.
2. Click *Prepare for {company}*.
3. Click *Generate a new brief*.
4. Read the brief. It starts from where your last coaching call with the company left off, then pulls together the last meeting and what it showed, commitments since, how the scorecard has moved, and anything needing attention. Click *Copy* to put it on your clipboard.

A brief that drew on a coaching call is shown only to the company's coaches.

The dialog shows your two most recent briefs for that company, so you can compare this week with last.

## Your coaching calls folder

This is where you set up call recordings for all the companies you coach. Calls with all your clients can live in one folder, such as Google Meet's *Meet Recordings*. AiMS reads it with your own Google account and files each call as a coaching call under one of your companies. A coaching call is seen only by the company's coaches and its AiMS Champion, on the *Coaching calls* tab of Meeting Summaries. Aimee never reads it.

1. Click *Connect Google* and sign in with the account your calls are recorded on.
2. Paste the folder's address and click *Use this folder*.
3. New calls come in on their own within about 15 minutes. *Check now* looks straight away. Calls already in the folder before you connected it aren't brought in.

How a call finds its company:

1. The file name holds one of the company's names. These are listed under *Names that identify this company* on the company's Connections tab.
2. Otherwise, someone on the call is a person at one of your companies, by their name or email.
3. Otherwise, or if it matches more than one company, it waits under *Calls to sort*.

Only the companies you coach are ever chosen.

*Change folder* removes the folder, so you can choose another. Calls already filed stay where they are.

## Calls to sort

Each call shows its date and who spoke on it.

- **File it.** Choose the company and click *File it*. It's summarised in a few minutes and appears on that company's *Coaching calls* tab.
- **Send future calls named "…" here too** is ticked for you. It adds that name to the company's names on its Connections tab, so the next call with it in the file name files itself.
- **Not a coaching call.** Choose it and click *Set aside*. The call is removed from the list and its words aren't kept.

*Filed automatically this week* lists the calls that found their company on their own, and why: "matched "Ridgeline" in the name" or "matched Dana Ruiz on the call". If one went to the wrong company, click *Move*, choose the right one and click *Move* again. It's summarised again for that company.

## How to work inside a company

Click the company's name in *Your companies* or on a *Needs your attention* row. The sidebar then shows that company's pages.

Only clicking a company moves you into it. Opening a page or a conversation from another company doesn't. If you coach just one company, you're taken into it automatically.

::: role system_admin
To step back out, use *Exit {company}* in the user menu.
:::

## Common questions

**Why can't I reassign a commitment or link it to a priority from My commitments?** Those need the company's own page. Open Commitments inside that company.

**Who else can see my coaching calls?** Only the company's other coaches and its AiMS Champion. Not the company's admins, and not a system admin unless they coach the company too.

**Can I use a company's own folder instead?** No. Folders and Teams meetings on a company's Connections tab bring in team meetings. Coaching calls come in through your coaching calls folder here, or by uploading one on the company's *Coaching calls* tab of Meeting Summaries.

::: role system_admin
**I have no companies assigned. Why the notice?** Coach HQ only shows companies you're assigned to coach, even for a system admin. Your access to every company is unchanged. To see your own commitments, open Commitments inside a company.
:::
