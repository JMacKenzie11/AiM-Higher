# Coach HQ

Your home base as an AiMS Coach. It covers only the companies you're assigned to coach, in four tabs: *Companies* (how each company is doing), *Coaching calls* (your calls, what was said, and your next session), *Your commitments* (your own commitments across every company) and *Connections* (where your call recordings come from).

## Companies

- **Your companies, first.** One row each: scorecard out of 10, Follow-Through Rate, priorities on track and overdue in the open quarter, the last team meeting with its facilitation score, and the last coaching call. The arrows compare with four weeks ago; "no change" means the number is the same. With no figure from four weeks ago, there's no arrow. A coaching call more than 14 days ago shows in amber. *Needs you* marks a company where something has slipped: a scorecard drop, a low or falling Follow-Through Rate, a weak facilitation review, a priority more than 14 days past due, or a meeting recording that looks like it belongs to that company. Up to two short reasons show under it, such as *Follow-through below 60%*. Companies that need you come first, the ones with the most slipping at the top, then the rest in alphabetical order. *Prepare* opens the session for that company.
- **Three numbers.** How many companies need you, how many of your own commitments are due this week, and how many calls are waiting to be analysed. Click one to go to it.
- **Recent activity.** The latest meetings summarised, reviews finished, and quarters opened or closed.

## Coaching calls

The tab shows how many calls are waiting, when any are.

- **Waiting to be analysed.** Calls from your folder that didn't find their company on their own.
- **Call summaries.** Every coaching call with your companies, newest first, with one sentence on what each was about. Choose a company to see only its calls and to prepare your next session with it. *Search* finds a call by a name or a topic. A call still being analysed says *Analysing*. *Open* shows the whole summary.

## Your commitments

Every commitment you own, across all your companies, with the company named on each row. Close out, reschedule and park work the same as on Commitments. The tab says how many are due this week, when any are.

## How to prepare a session

1. Click *Prepare* on the company's row in *Your companies*. Coaching calls opens on that company and scrolls to its next session. Or open *Coaching calls* and click the company's name.
2. *Your next session with {company}* shows three things:
   - **Since your last call.** How follow-through moved, any priority that became overdue, the team meetings held (with commitments, issues and facilitation score), and new issues. With no coaching call yet, it looks back four weeks and says so.
   - **Where you left off.** The decisions from your last call with the company, and your own open commitments to them. Click *Mark done* when you've kept one.
   - **Coaching notes**, across the bottom of the card. Click *Write coaching notes*. It takes a few seconds. The notes follow the AiMS coaching principles, in four parts: *What's working* (strengths and wins since your last call), *What they set out to do* (what the leader said they'd try on that call, and what has happened since), *Worth exploring* (one or two things to look into, starting from what the leader wants and how the work is set up), and *Questions to open with* (three questions to start the session). Click *Copy* to put them on your clipboard, or *Write new notes* to start again.

   When a new coaching call or team meeting comes in after the notes were written, the notes are put away and the card says what's new since them, with *Write coaching notes* to write fresh ones.
3. *Work inside {company}* takes you into the company.

Coaching notes that drew on a coaching call are shown only to the company's coaches.

## Upload a call

1. On *Coaching calls*, click *Upload a call* on the *Waiting to be analysed* card.
2. Choose the company the call was with, and the day it was held. A name is optional.
3. Click *Choose file*, pick the .docx, .txt or .vtt transcript, and click *Add call*.

It's analysed in a few minutes and appears in *Call summaries*. You can upload calls only for companies you coach.

## Connections: your call recordings folder

The *Connections* tab is where you set up call recordings for all the companies you coach. Calls with all your clients can live in one folder, such as Google Meet's *Meet Recordings*. AiMS reads it with your own Google account and brings in only the calls with "coaching" in their title, so keep that word in the meeting name. Each one is filed as a coaching call under one of your companies. A coaching call is seen only by the company's coaches and its AiMS Champion. Aimee never reads it.

Once your folder is connected, the tab shows one line with the folder and when it was last checked. *Check now* looks straight away, and *Settings* shows the Google account and *Change folder*.

1. Click *Connect Google* and sign in with the account your calls are recorded on. You come back to *Connections*.
2. Open the folder in Google Drive, copy its address from your browser, paste it in and click *Use this folder*.
3. New calls come in on their own within about 15 minutes. Calls already in the folder before you connected it aren't brought in.

How AiMS-HQ knows which company a call is with. It checks each new call in this order:

1. **The file name.** If it includes a company's name, the call goes to that company. You can add other names a company goes by under *Names that identify this company* on its Connections tab.
2. **The people on the call.** If someone on it works at one of your companies, by name or email, the call goes there.
3. **You pick.** If neither one settles it, or the call could belong to two companies, it waits under *Waiting to be analysed* for you to choose.

Calls only ever go to companies you coach.

*Change folder* (under *Settings*) removes the folder, so you can choose another. Calls already filed stay where they are. To disconnect Google, change folder first, then click *Disconnect Google*.

## Waiting to be analysed

Calls AiMS-HQ couldn't match to a company on its own wait here for you to pick the company. Once you do, the call is analysed, and only you, the company's other coaches and its AiMS Champion will see it.

Each call shows its date, how long it ran when the transcript says, and who was on it.

- **AiMS's best guess.** When AiMS can tell which company it is, the card says so and why ("Looks like Ridgeline Electric: Sam Patel was on the call"), and that company is already chosen. When people from two of your companies were on it, the card asks which one it's for.
- **Analyse.** Check the company and click *Analyse*. A line confirms it's being analysed for that company, and it appears in *Call summaries* in a few minutes.
- **Remember for next time.** Under the company choice is a word from the call's file name, such as the company's name, with a tick beside it. When you click *Analyse*, that word is remembered for the company: from then on, any call with that word in its file name goes straight to that company. Change the word if AiMS-HQ picked the wrong one, or untick it to skip. Your own name is never remembered, because it's in every call. The words are listed, and can be removed, under *Names that identify this company* on the company's Connections tab.
- **Not a coaching call.** Click it to set the call aside. The call is removed from the list and its words aren't kept.

*Filed automatically this week* lists the calls that found their company on their own, and why. If one went to the wrong company, click *Move*, choose the right one and click *Move* again. It's summarised again for that company.

## How to work inside a company

Click the company's name in *Your companies*, or *Work inside {company}* on the session card. The sidebar then shows that company's pages.

Only clicking a company moves you into it. Opening a page or a conversation from another company doesn't. If you coach just one company, you're taken into it automatically.

::: role system_admin
To step back out, use *Exit {company}* in the user menu.
:::

## Common questions

**Why can't I reassign a commitment or link it to a priority from Your commitments?** Those need the company's own page. Open Commitments inside that company.

**Who else can see my coaching calls?** Only the company's other coaches and its AiMS Champion. Not the company's admins, and not a system admin unless they coach the company too.

**Can I use a company's own folder instead?** No. Folders and Teams meetings on a company's Connections tab bring in team meetings. Coaching calls come in through your folder here, by *Upload a call* on *Coaching calls*, or by uploading one on the company's *Coaching calls* tab of Meeting Summaries.

**Where does "In a sentence" come from?** It's the first sentence of the call's own summary. Open the call to read the rest.

::: role system_admin
**I have no companies assigned. Why the notice?** Coach HQ only shows companies you're assigned to coach, even for a system admin. Your access to every company is unchanged. To see your own commitments, open Commitments inside a company.
:::
